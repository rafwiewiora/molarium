# 7KPA high-disruption native validation — 2026-08-23

## Decision summary

Five hash-selected poses from the 24-pose high-disruption panel were rescored on one NVIDIA L4.
The selected set contains the two browser-feasible pyrazole poses, the two browser-feasible
tetrahydropyran poses, and the single distinct spiro-ketone negative control.

| Comparison | Fixed gate | Observed range | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Browser ANI-2x vs native TorchANI ANI-2x | ≤0.1 kcal/mol and force relative RMS ≤1e-3 | 0.00277–0.06312 kcal/mol; 3.06e-6–3.48e-6 | **PASS** |
| Native OpenMM 8.2 Reference vs CUDA double | ≤1e-3 kJ/mol and force relative RMS ≤1e-3 | 4.29e-5–1.11e-4 kJ/mol; 1.21e-6–1.30e-6 | **PASS** |
| Browser Sage WebGPU vs bundled OpenMM/WASM Reference | ≤0.01 kcal/mol and force relative RMS ≤1e-3 | 4.63e-5–2.76e-4 kcal/mol; 1.01e-5–1.16e-5 | **PASS** |
| Bundled OpenMM/WASM vs native OpenMM 8.2 Reference | ≤0.01 kcal/mol and force relative RMS ≤1e-3 | 3.849–4.757 kcal/mol; 0.0423–0.0444 | **FAIL** |
| Browser Sage WebGPU vs native OpenMM 8.2 CUDA | ≤0.01 kcal/mol and force relative RMS ≤1e-3 | 3.849–4.757 kcal/mol; 0.0423–0.0444 | **FAIL** |

The two internally independent pairs agree: browser WebGPU agrees with the bundled browser
OpenMM oracle, and native OpenMM Reference agrees with CUDA. The cross-runtime Sage comparison does
not. Atom order, coordinates, topology and the numeric System were hash-gated; browser/native
ANI-2x parity on those same pose packets also passed. The source of the remaining browser-OpenMM to
native-OpenMM discrepancy is unresolved. These results therefore do **not** support a claim that the
browser Sage energies or forces are native-OpenMM-equivalent for this panel.

## Shortlist results

All energy deltas compare identical molecular graphs and coordinates. ANI and MMFF relaxation drops
are isolated-ligand strain diagnostics, not binding scores.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Spiro ketone / 0 | 0.00277 | 3.48e-6 | 36.845 | 0.203 | 41.985 |
| Pyrazole / 0 | 0.02302 | 3.34e-6 | 28.419 | 0.069 | 34.412 |
| Pyrazole / 1 | 0.02302 | 3.24e-6 | 28.419 | 0.069 | 34.412 |
| Tetrahydropyran / 0 | 0.06312 | 3.06e-6 | 31.636 | 0.163 | 33.533 |
| Tetrahydropyran / 1 | 0.03426 | 3.14e-6 | 34.164 | 0.150 | 36.014 |

The spiro pose is not merely clashing with the receptor. Its isolated-ligand ANI-2x relaxation drops
36.8 kcal/mol and moves the heavy atoms by 0.203 Å, while MMFF94 drops 42.0 kcal/mol. It is therefore
intrinsically strained as drawn. The earlier browser receptor diagnostics (11 clashes and a strongly
positive Lennard-Jones term) show that receptor clash is still the larger pose-level warning.
