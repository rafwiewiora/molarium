# 7KPA high-disruption native validation — 2026-08-23

## Decision summary

Five hash-selected poses from the 24-pose high-disruption panel were rescored on one NVIDIA L4.
The selected set contains the two browser-feasible pyrazole poses, the two browser-feasible
tetrahydropyran poses, and the single distinct spiro-ketone negative control.

| Comparison | Fixed gate | Observed range | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Browser ANI-2x vs native TorchANI ANI-2x | ≤0.1 kcal/mol and force relative RMS ≤1e-3 | 0.00277–0.06312 kcal/mol; 3.06e-6–3.48e-6 | **PASS** |
| Native OpenMM 8.2 Reference vs CUDA double | ≤1e-3 kJ/mol and force relative RMS ≤1e-3 | 4.29e-5–1.11e-4 kJ/mol; 1.21e-6–1.30e-6 | **PASS** |
| Rebuilt OpenMM 8.2 WASM vs native OpenMM 8.2 Reference | ≤0.01 kcal/mol and force relative RMS ≤1e-3 | 0–2.84e-14 kcal/mol; 2.68e-15–3.76e-15 | **PASS** |
| Browser Sage WebGPU vs rebuilt OpenMM/WASM Reference | ≤0.01 kcal/mol and force relative RMS ≤1e-3 | 4.07e-5–1.24e-4 kcal/mol; 1.01e-5–1.16e-5 | **PASS** |

The cross-runtime gate passes. The checked OpenMM binary was rebuilt from the pinned official
OpenMM 8.2.0 archive, and the same Molarium C bridge was also compiled against a native OpenMM 8.2
library. Native and WASM energies and every force component agree to floating-point noise. A fresh
Chrome run then scored the identical five packet poses in explicit vacuum, without constraints or a
cutoff; its numeric-System hashes equal the packet hashes for all five poses. Browser Sage WebGPU
agrees with that rebuilt WASM oracle within the preregistered thresholds. Together these two
independent comparisons support native-OpenMM equivalence for this fixed five-pose panel.

The earlier apparent 3.849–4.757 kcal/mol disagreement was a protocol error: the recorded browser
single points used OBC2 implicit solvent, while the native comparison used vacuum. Rerunning the
five browser poses with explicit OBC2 reproduces every earlier browser energy exactly; explicit
vacuum produces the native energies. Direct vacuum scoring with the prior WASM hash
`1711ccdae3f89b1667223d6e47182c5bbed05c78f6efbdd472ae700a93200a97` also matches the rebuilt
WASM energies exactly for all five poses. The rebuild supplied missing provenance and exact-byte
loading, but did not numerically repair the OpenMM kernel. The validation harness now records and
asserts solvent, constraints, cutoff, System, coordinate, packet and binary identity.

## Shortlist results

All energy deltas compare identical molecular graphs and coordinates. ANI and MMFF relaxation drops
are isolated-ligand strain diagnostics, not binding scores.

| Tetrahydropyran / 1 | 0.03426 | 3.14e-6 | 34.164 | 0.150 | 36.014 |

The spiro pose is not merely clashing with the receptor. Its isolated-ligand ANI-2x relaxation drops
36.8 kcal/mol and moves the heavy atoms by 0.203 Å, while MMFF94 drops 42.0 kcal/mol. It is therefore
intrinsically strained as drawn. The earlier browser receptor diagnostics (11 clashes and a strongly
positive Lennard-Jones term) show that receptor clash is still the larger pose-level warning.
